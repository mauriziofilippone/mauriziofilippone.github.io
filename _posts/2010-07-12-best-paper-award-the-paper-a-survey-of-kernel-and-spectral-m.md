---
title: "Best Paper Award - The paper 'A survey of kernel and spectral methods for clustering' has been chose"
date: 2010-07-12
categories: news
---

Best Paper Award - The paper "A survey of kernel and spectral methods for clustering" has been chosen to be the best paper published in 2008 in the journal *Pattern Recognition*. The award will be presented at [ICPR 2010](http://www.icpr2010.org/index.php) in Istanbul ([PR homepage](http://www.elsevier.com/wps/find/journaldescription.cws_home/328/description#description)) ([link](http://dx.doi.org/10.1016/j.patcog.2010.08.001))
